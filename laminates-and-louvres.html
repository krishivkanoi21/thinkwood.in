05 — Straight Answers

Questions we get every week.

Can these louvres and panels be used outdoors?

All seven ranges (Snazzy, Trendy, Woodvents, Groove in Wood, Elemet, Cressida, HDHMR) are water and termite resistant, so they hold up in kitchens, wash areas and covered balconies. They're not a facade-grade product for fully exposed exterior walls — for open-air facades and boundary screens, ask us about the aluminium and WPC options on our cladding range.

What's the difference between Super Matt and Surface Matters?

Super Matt is a bendable polymer laminate in 24 solid colours, built for shutter edges and 180° wraps where a joint would otherwise show. Surface Matters is the full decorative book — 464 designs across woodgrains, stone and solids — for everything that isn't a curved or wrapped edge.

Does Originals MCS need edge banding?

Yes — a matching 25 mm edge band is available for every finish. Super Matt is the one range in this book that doesn't need banding, since the polymer itself wraps a 180° return.

What's the difference between Woodvents and Groove in Wood?

Both are reeded L-8/L-15/L-16 profile panels, but Woodvents cuts a deeper, wider-pitched groove built to read across a full-height wall, while Groove in Wood is a tighter, shallower reed closer to traditional beadboard — better suited to shutters and headboards seen up close.

What is the Mineral Composite Sheet (MCS)?

A flat 1.5 mm sheet in the same finish family as the Snazzy and Trendy louvres, used to fill in flat wall sections between fluted panels so a room reads as one material at different depths rather than a patchwork.

Do you supply only, or install as well?

Both. Most clients take supply plus installation through our applicators so material and workmanship stay with one party. Supply-only is available if your carpenter or contractor is already appointed.

How long from order to delivery?

Stock profiles ship within 3–7 working days within Kolkata. Custom colours and larger production runs carry a 2–4 week lead time. You'll get an accurate date in writing before the order is released.

Is there a minimum order?

There's no hard minimum — a single feature wall, partition or skirting run is viable. Custom colour batches on any profile do carry a practical minimum, and we'll flag that upfront.

Can we see the material before ordering?

Yes. Visit the Experience Centre at 13C Paul Mansions, 6 Bishop Lefroy Road, Mon–Sat 10 AM to 7 PM, to see full panels and profiles mounted. Call ahead so we can set aside time for you.
