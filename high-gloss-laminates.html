03 — Straight Answers

Questions people ask first.

What does a sheet cost?

₹1,650 per sheet including GST, for a full 8 × 4 ft sheet at 1 mm. Call or WhatsApp us with the number of sheets you need.

Which colours are available?

Message us on WhatsApp and we'll send photos of the colours in stock, or come and see the sheets at our Experience Centre.

Can I buy just a few sheets?

Yes. We sell single sheets for a home project as well as larger quantities for carpenters, contractors and interior designers.

Is it suitable for a kitchen?

Yes. The polymer sheet is waterproof, so it handles kitchen moisture and humid weather without swelling or lifting.

What does "unicore" mean?

The colour runs right through the core of the sheet rather than sitting only on the surface, so cut edges and grooves keep the same colour instead of showing a dark line.
