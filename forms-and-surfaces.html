04 — Straight Answers

Questions we get every week.

What exactly is Bonded Metal?

Metal granules cast into a tough fibre-reinforced polymer matrix, so the face is genuine aluminium, bronze or nickel silver — it patinas, it feels cold to the touch, and it can be refinished — but the panel weighs a fraction of solid plate. That weight difference is what makes it viable on a lift car door or a hung wall panel where sheet metal would be too heavy and too flat.

How is Bonded Quartz different from Bonded Metal?

Same casting process and the same sculptural relief, but the filler is ceramic microspheres rather than metal granules. That gives you a bright white or charcoal surface instead of a metal one, at a similar weight, and it wipes clean — which is why it tends to get specified for hospitals, hotels and pool halls.

Can you re-skin an existing lift?

Yes, and it's most of this work. Door skins and cabin panels are made to your existing car and door sizes and fixed over what's already there, so the lift is out of service for days rather than the weeks a full car replacement takes.

Is the decorative glass structural?

It's laminated glass, so it gets specified like any other laminated architectural glass — partitions, doors, balustrades and screens. Send us the opening size and what it's doing and we'll confirm the make-up and thickness for that application.

How do the metal finishes age?

Dark and patina finishes are applied chemically, so they're part of the surface rather than a coating sitting on top — they can be touched up in place if a panel gets marked. Natural finishes will keep developing their own patina over time unless they're sealed, which some clients specifically want.

Are these stocked or made to order?

Made to order against your sizes and chosen pattern, so lead time depends on the range and the quantity. You'll get a date in writing before the order is released rather than an estimate that moves.

Do you supply only, or install as well?

Both. Most clients take supply plus installation through our own fit-out crew so material and workmanship stay with one party. Supply-only is available if your contractor is already appointed.

Yes. Visit the Experience Centre at 13C Paul Mansions, 6 Bishop Lefroy Road, Mon–Sat 10 AM to 7 PM, to handle the metal, glass and wood samples in person. Call ahead so we can set aside time and pull the patterns you're interested in.
