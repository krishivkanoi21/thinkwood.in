04 — Straight Answers

Questions we get every week.

What does flooring cost per square foot?

It moves with the range and the plank size more than anything else — an 8 mm Elegance Walnut board and a 12 mm herringbone are not in the same bracket, and levelling, underlay, skirting and beading sit on top of the material rate. Share the area and a photo of the existing subfloor and you'll get a built-up figure rather than a range.

What is hybrid laminate, and how is it different from ordinary laminate?

Hybrid — sometimes called rigid core — uses a black HDF core impregnated with resins and carbon fibre instead of a standard fibreboard core. It behaves far better around spills and humidity than conventional laminate while keeping the same finish and the same click installation. Every range on this page is built on that core.

Which range survives Kolkata's humidity best?

All eight are AC5 with a moisture-resistant core, so none of them is a bad answer here. Livanti carries a 100-hour water test and is the only one rated for underfloor heating; the Pietra collection carries a 12-hour water-protection warranty. For a kitchen or a flat where a balcony door will eventually let water in, those are the two we'd point at first.

Wide plank or herringbone — how do I choose?

Wide plank reads calmer and makes a large room feel larger; Majesty Grande at 1845 × 235 mm is the widest board we stock. Herringbone reads richer and busier and suits smaller, more formal rooms — and because the blocks are short, it wastes less around awkward corners. Use the filters above to see each set on its own.

Can these go in a kitchen or a bathroom?

Kitchens, yes — the hybrid core is built for exactly that. A bathroom with standing water we would still steer you away from; water-resistant and fully waterproof are not the same claim, and we'd rather say so now than argue about it later.

Do you have to remove the existing tiles?

Usually not. These go down as a floating click system over sound, level tile with an underlay, which saves the demolition and the dust. If the tile is drummy or badly out of level we'll say so at the site measure rather than lay over a problem.

Do you supply only, or install as well?

Both. Most clients take supply plus installation through our fitters so material and workmanship stay with one party. Supply-only is available if your contractor is already appointed.
